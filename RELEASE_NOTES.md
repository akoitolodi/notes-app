## What's New in Notes by Bai v1.7.0

### School Planner & Canvas LMS Integration (Zero Admin Token Needed!)
- **Unified School Planner & Canvas Hub**: Interactive Month Calendar with Exam Countdown banner, combined Personal + Canvas To-Do list, Weekly Class Timetable (Mon–Sat), **GWA (PH 1.00–5.00) / GPA (US 4.00) Target Final Exam Calculator**, and a **16-Week Study Activity Heatmap**.
- **Works Even When Schools Restrict Canvas Tokens**: Connects directly to your personal **Canvas Calendar Feed (`.ics`)** (using native `CapacitorHttp` on Android for zero CORS issues!), supports optional **Course Announcement RSS feeds**, and includes a **1-Tap Quick-Paste Announcement Importer** that auto-detects due dates. Tap **Start Note** on any Canvas item to create a pre-filled workspace note automatically.

### Study Commute Audio Mode & Reverse Feynman Roleplay
- **Study Commute Audio Player**: Tap the **Headphones** icon on any non-vault note for offline **Read Aloud** (with LaTeX-to-speech cleaning) or a **2-Voice AI Study Podcast** (`Host` & `Tutor`), plus **Hands-Free Audio Flashcards** inside Daily Review (reads Question → thinking countdown → reads Answer → auto-advances).
- **"Teach the AI" (Reverse Feynman)**: New **Teach AI** tab in Study Mode where AI plays a curious 1st-year student asking probing questions, then grades your Clarity & Accuracy and syncs weak concepts into DeepTutor Learner Memory.

### CS, Math & Offline Zero-Cloud Superpowers
- **Algorithm & Data Structure Visualizer** (`Learn Coding → Visual` & catalog banner): 100% offline animated **Sorting** (Bubble, Insertion, Merge, QuickSort), **Binary Search**, interactive wall-drawing **BFS / DFS / Dijkstra Pathfinding**, and **Recursion Call-Stack** (`factorial`, `fibonacci`).
- **Offline 2D Math Function Grapher** (`Dev & IT Toolkit → Grapher`): Zero-`eval` math parser plotting up to 3 color-coded equations with zoom/pan, `f(x)` & `f'(x)` evaluator, root finder, and 1-tap **Save Graph to Note**.
- **"Bugs I Solved" Stack-Trace Debugger** (`Dev & IT Toolkit → Bug Log`): Instant offline parser for Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, C/C++, Java/Kotlin, and Gradle errors + optional AI root-cause fix + searchable local archive.
- **BaiDrop Offline Share & Local Wi-Fi PC↔Phone Sync**: Export/import portable `.bai.json` note bundles or copyable `BAIDROP1:` codes, or sync unlocked notes over your home/campus Wi-Fi (strictly excluding Hidden Vault & `Passwords & Keys`).
- **OLED True-Black Theme & Paper Textures**: Choose **OLED** appearance and **Blank / Ruled / Dot Grid / Graph** note paper styles in Settings.
