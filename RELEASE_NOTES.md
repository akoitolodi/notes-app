## What's New in Notes by Bai v1.8.1

### Smart Past-Assignment Auto-Archive & 1-Tap "Mark Overdue / Submitted Done"
- **Auto-Archive Past Canvas Assignments on Import**: Canvas `.ics` feeds export every assignment from the whole semester without submission/grade status. Newly synced assignments whose due date is already in the past are now archived automatically as Done instead of flagging old already-graded work as Overdue (you can still uncheck any item inside **Show Completed** if you need to submit it late).
- **1-Tap `✓ Mark Overdue Done` & `✓ Clear Past`**: Added a 1-tap button on both the **Home Reminder Banner** and **School Planner → To-Dos & Canvas** so already-submitted/graded assignments clear instantly and stay permanently marked Done across every future Canvas auto-sync.
- **Instant Home Badge Refresh**: Checking off any assignment immediately updates the Folders Home badge and clears the persistent Android sticky notification.

> Includes all `v1.8.0` features (Canvas-style Course Dashboard, AI-indexed Modules & Files for `.pdf`/`.pptx`/`.docx`/Canvas Pages/Multi-OCR, Course AI Tutor & Exam Builder, Android Share Target, Persistent Due-Date & Announcement Notifications, and Adaptive Slow Internet Mode).
>
> **Update Note**: Install `Notes-by-Bai.apk` directly over your current version (do not uninstall).
