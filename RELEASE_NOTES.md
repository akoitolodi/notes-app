## What's New in Notes by Bai v2.0.9

### 1. Seamless Edge-to-Edge Status Bar + Optional Fullscreen Toggle
- **Removed Top Black Strip**: App themes — including **Aurora Glass**, **Frosted Glass**, and dark presets — now extend continuously behind your clock, notification icons, and bottom navigation pill instead of cutting off cleanly below a black status bar strip, while still padding ONCE natively for the keyboard (zero bottom gap).
- **Hide Status Bar (Fullscreen)**: Added an optional **Hide status bar (Fullscreen)** toggle under **Settings → Appearance** to hide the Android clock and notification icons (swipe down from the top to peek anytime).

### 2. Full PowerPoint (`.ppt` + `.pptx`) Offline Upgrade & 1-Tap External Open
- **100% Offline Legacy `.ppt` Support**: Built a pure-JS OLE2/CFBF `[MS-PPT]` parser so older PowerPoint `.ppt` handouts open right inside the in-app Document Viewer and import cleanly into Notes & Course Modules (plus Android `"Open With"` support).
- **Richer `.pptx` Slide Rendering**: Upgraded slide cards to display PowerPoint **tables**, **bold / italic / underlined & indented sub-bullets**, **SmartArt & Chart text labels**, slide accent bars, and a **16:9 presentation framing** toggle.
- **1-Tap Open in External App**: Added an **Open in External App** button in the Document Viewer to hand off any PDF, PPT/PPTX, or DOCX straight to Microsoft PowerPoint, Google Slides, or WPS via Android `FileProvider`.

> **Update Note**: Install `Notes-by-Bai.apk` directly over your current version (do not uninstall).
