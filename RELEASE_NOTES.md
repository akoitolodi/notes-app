## What's New in Notes by Bai v1.9.0

### Phone-First Material Capture (Android)
- **Android `Share` & `Open With` → Direct to Course + Module**: Share or open any `.pdf`, `.pptx`, `.docx`, screenshot, or Canvas/Google Chat link from Chrome, Canvas Student, Google Chat, Messenger, or Android Files straight into a specific **Course Module** (AI-indexed immediately!) or regular Note folder.
- **Multi-File Batch Upload**: Pick multiple `.pdf`, `.pptx`, and `.docx` files at once from your phone's Downloads folder and index all of them into a Course Module in one go.
- **1-Tap Public Google Docs / Slides Link Importer**: Import `"Anyone with the link"` Google Docs & Google Slides straight into your Course Modules right on your phone.

### 6 New Canvas-Style Course Superpowers
1. **Per-Course Grades, Canvas "What-If" Simulator & Semester GWA/GPA**: Dedicated **`Grades`** tab inside every Course with customizable syllabus weights, score tracking, live **What-If Mode** (*"What if I score 90% on Finals?"*), and an automatic **Semester Standing (PH GWA + US GPA)** rollup on Course Dashboard.
2. **Auto-Pinned "Course Links" Hub**: Automatically extracts Google Chat, Google Drive, Google Docs/Slides, Meet/Zoom, and GitHub links from each course's announcements onto Course Home (with 1-tap **Import** on public Google Docs/Slides).
3. **Assignment Status Workflow + Pre-Submit AI Rubric Checker**: Track assignments across `Not Started → In Progress → Submitted → Graded` and check your draft against your teacher's rubric with AI **before** submitting on Canvas.
4. **Module Study Progress (`3/5 studied`)**: Mark uploaded PDFs/slides as **Studied** with progress bars on each Module, Course Home, and Course Card.
5. **Course Nicknames, Edit Card & Semester Archive**: Give long course titles clean nicknames, edit colors/instructors/RSS URLs anytime, and archive finished semesters cleanly.
6. **Customizable Attendance / Absence Guard (PH CHED 20% Rule)**: Quick `MWF (10) / TTh (7) / 1×/wk (3)` presets or your exact syllabus max + configurable `3 Lates = 1 Absent` conversion and date log.

### UI Design Polish
- **Fixed White Button Boxes**: Styled `.icon-btn`, frosted `.course-due-pill`, and explicit `appearance: none` on Calendar `<` `>` arrows, Course checkboxes, Course Card controls, Grapher controls, and sheet close icons — plus made custom note paper textures (`Ruled / Dot Grid / Graph`) render directly on note pages.

> **Update Note**: Install `Notes-by-Bai.apk` directly over your current version (do not uninstall) — all notes, vault records, courses, schedules, and API keys stay 100% intact.
